## How It Works, A Simple Four Step Process

The process is simple and requires minimal training. First, an instructor uploads an exam document in any common format. Second, the AI extracts the questions, detects question types, identifies answer options, and restructures the content into a standardized digital format. Third, the instructor reviews and edits the questions, sets the exam duration, and schedules or starts the exam. Fourth, students take the exam on their mobile devices; objective questions are graded automatically, AI assists with short answers and essays, and the final reports are generated immediately. Throughout this workflow, the instructor remains in control no content is published without review, and all AI scores can be overridden if needed.

## AI That Works For You, Not Against You

Smart Exam Taker uses AI only where it adds clear value, leaving deterministic tasks to the system. For question conversion, uploading a Word or PDF exam triggers AI extraction that identifies questions, types, answer options, and marks, then restructures everything into a standardized digital format in minutes. For short answer and essay grading, AI evaluates student responses using the question, any reference answer, and the grading rubric, then suggests a score and detailed feedback that instructors can review and override. Objective questions multiple choice, true/false, matching, and fill in the blank are graded deterministically with one hundred percent accuracy, with no AI involved. All AI generated grading details are stored and visible to instructors, ensuring transparency and trust. This is not about replacing teachers; it’s about giving them back their time to focus on teaching and mentoring.
